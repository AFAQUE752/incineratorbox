🎮 SERVER FEATURES

Explore our unique game mechanics and progression systems!

⛏️

8 Overworld Mines

Start at the Wood Mine and grind your way up through 8 progressively harder mines. Each mine rewards better resources — the further you go, the stronger you become.

🗺️ Mine Progression (Easiest → Hardest):
1 🪵 Wood Mine Starter
2 🪨 Stone Mine Basic
3 🖤 Coal Mine Common
4 🔩 Iron Mine Uncommon
5 ✨ Gold Mine Rare
6 💎 Diamond Mine Epic
7 💚 Emerald Mine Legendary
8 🔷 Lapis Mine Endgame
🪙

Aurex Currency

Forget boring vanilla currency. Incinerator uses Aurex — a fully custom currency system built from the ground up. Grind mines to earn fragments, then refine them into increasingly powerful forms.

📈 Aurex Progression:
1 ⚗️ Aurex Fragment Raw
2 🥇 Aurex Bar Refined
3 🟡 Aurex Block Compressed
4 ✨ Pure Aurex Elite
5 👑 Crowned Aurex Endgame
  • Fragments drop from all mines; higher mines yield more
  • Refine fragments into Bars, Blocks, and beyond
  • Spend Aurex on upgrades, crates & market
📦

Crate System

Crack open crates to score exclusive loot you can't get anywhere else. Earn keys through grinding, events, and rewards — then spin for a chance at rare gear and goodies.

🎁 What Crates Offer:
  • Exclusive items not available in shops
  • Rare gear and combat upgrades
  • Aurex bonuses and special rewards
  • Keys earned through gameplay & events
⚔️

Gear Upgrades

Power up your armor and weapons through a fully custom upgrading system. The stronger the mine you grind, the better gear you can work towards. More details coming with the overhaul!

🔥 What's Being Built:
  • Advanced custom upgrading system
  • Rare Aurex materials required for top-tier gear
  • Multiple overworld armour sets with harder unlock requirements
  • Rewarding progression from weak to unstoppable
⚠️ Full upgrade costs & details revealed after the reset

🔥 Bosses & Combat

The Crucible breeds monsters. Only the strongest will survive — let alone conquer them.

🔨
The Forger
Master of Fire & Steel
Born from the molten core of The Crucible, The Forger is a colossus of burning metal and brute force. Its hammer strikes crack the arena floor and its armour is forged from the rarest materials in the world. A gateway boss — defeat it to prove your early-game dominance.
🔥 Moderate Challenge
🧊
Glacial Tyrant
Sovereign of Frozen Ruin
A titan encased in ancient ice, the Glacial Tyrant slows everything around it and punishes reckless fighters. Its frost aura freezes under-geared players in their tracks. Mid-game players who've mastered the mines will find this a true test of their build.
❄️ Hard Challenge
🌀
Cyro Sentinel
The Endgame Warden
The pinnacle of boss encounters on Incinerator. The Cyro Sentinel is a fusion of cryo energy and arcane power — unpredictable, merciless, and designed to humble even the most maxed-out players. Only those wielding Crowned Aurex gear stand a real chance.
💀 Extreme — Endgame

Bosses drop exclusive loot, rare Aurex materials, and crate keys not obtainable anywhere else. Each boss is designed to gate progression — don't expect to brute-force them unprepared. More bosses are in development, with Nether-specific encounters planned for a future expansion.

⚖️ SERVER RULES

Read carefully. Breaking rules may result in warnings, kicks, or permanent bans. Play fair, fight hard, and dominate the arena!

Rules 1–9: General Conduct

Be respectful to all players.
No harassment, hate speech, or discrimination.
No spamming chat, excessive caps, or flooding.
Keep usernames and skins appropriate.
English only in global chat.
No cheating, hacks, or exploits.
Do not sabotage matches intentionally.
No glitching arenas, items, or mechanics.
Use only allowed mods/plugins.

Rules 10–17: Gameplay

PvP is fair play: no teaming in solo modes.
Respect arena mechanics and boundaries.
No blocking spawn areas or traps.
No camping in unfair locations.
Follow mini-game specific rules.
Do not abuse teleport commands.
No scamming or stealing from other players.
No item or Aurex duplication.

Rules 18–25: Economy & Community

Use server shops and economy fairly.
No advertising other servers.
Follow instructions from staff members.
Do not argue publicly with staff.
Report bugs, exploits, or rule breakers properly.
False reporting may result in punishment.
Avoid inappropriate or offensive builds.
Have fun and maintain a positive community environment.
